Ride the face of the wave. Steer up and down with your mouse, finger or arrow keys. Tap or press Space to pop an air: launch near the lip for a trick. When the wave barrels, stay mid-face to get tubed. Dodge driftwood and reef rocks, grab shells and power-ups, and ride through each project ring to unlock it. You have 3 lives.
