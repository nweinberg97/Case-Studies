Drop in

Steer with your mouse, finger or arrow keys. Tap or press Space to jump over rocks. Hit the JUMP waves for big-air tricks. Grab shells and power-ups for points. Every points milestone unlocks a project: surf through it to open it. You have 3 lives.

Slice it open

Projects get tossed into the air. Swipe across them (or click and drag) to slice. Slice the same project 3 times to unlock it. Bad-design bombs fly up too: Comic Sans, Lorem ipsum, Scope creep. Slice one and you lose a life. You have 3.

Spot the logo

Kits Beach, 5pm. Each round has a wanted project with three copies of its logo hidden behind trees, logs and boats. Snap all three before the sun sets. Other logos are decoys: tapping one costs 5 seconds.
